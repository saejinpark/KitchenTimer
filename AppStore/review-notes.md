Kitchen Timer (주방 타이머) — App Review Notes

The app has no account registration, login, user-generated content, or paid content.

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Kitchen Timer lets people run several cooking timers at once from Apple Watch. Users save their usual dishes and cooking times on iPhone once; on the watch they tap a dish to start it, and every running timer is shown in one list sorted by what finishes next.
- Problem it solves: when cooking several dishes at the same time, a single phone timer is not enough and hands are often wet or busy. The watch shows which dish needs attention, vibrates until it is acknowledged, and for multi-step dishes (e.g. steak: front side, then back side) it waits for the user to flip and confirm before timing the next step.
- Audience: anyone who cooks — home cooks and cooks in busy kitchens. It is a general consumer app available to everyone, not built for a specific business or organization.

3. SETUP AND HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No login, account, or sample file is needed. The app is free and has no in-app purchases.
- iPhone: open the app to see 4 sample dishes (라면/ramen, 파스타/pasta, 반숙 계란/soft-boiled egg, 스테이크/steak). Tap a dish to edit its icon, color, and cooking steps; tap + to add a dish. Changes sync automatically to the paired Apple Watch.
- Apple Watch: open the app and allow notifications. Swipe up (or tap +) to see the dishes and tap one to start a timer. Tap a running timer to see its ring; turn the Digital Crown to adjust by 10 seconds or tap the 60-second button to add a minute.
- Multi-step dish (스테이크): when a step ends, a red row appears at the top ("앞면 끝 → 뒷면 3:00" = "front done → back 3:00") and counts the time since it ended. Tap the row (or double tap) after flipping, and the next step starts counting from that moment.
- When a dish is done, it stays at the top as a red row, vibrates until it is acknowledged, and tapping the row acknowledges it.
- Optional "식당 모드" (restaurant mode) at the bottom of the dish list on the watch asks which burner number a pot is on, so identical dishes can be told apart.
- Siri on Apple Watch: "주방 타이머에서 파스타 시작해" (start pasta), "주방 타이머 남은 시간 알려줘" (time remaining).
Tip for faster review: on iPhone, edit a dish and set its time to a few seconds to see the finish alert quickly.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app uses no servers, data providers, authentication, payment, analytics, advertising, or AI services. It only uses Apple frameworks on the device: WatchConnectivity (iPhone → Apple Watch menu sync), UserNotifications (local notifications), WKExtendedRuntimeSession smart alarm (repeating haptics until acknowledged; WKBackgroundModes: alarm), and App Intents (Siri). No personal data is collected.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in all regions. The interface is in Korean.
